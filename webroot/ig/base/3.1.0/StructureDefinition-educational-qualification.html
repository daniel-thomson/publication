Extension: EducationalQualification

Official URL: http://hl7.org.nz/fhir/StructureDefinition/educational-qualification Version: 1.0.0
Active as of 2026-07-24 Computable Name: EducationalQualification

Educational qualifications a practitioner has. These may or may not be related to registration information.

Context of Use

    This extension may be used on the following element(s)

    • Element ID: Practitioner
    • Element ID: Practitioner.qualification

Example of extension

  ...
  <qualification>
    <extension url="http://hl7.org.nz/fhir/StructureDefinition/educational-qualification">
      <extension url="fullname">
        <valueString value="Fellow of the Royal Australian and New Zealand College of Psychiatrists qualification"/>
      </extension>
      <extension url="shortname">
        <valueString value="Psychiatrist fellow"/>
      </extension>
      <extension url="year">
        <valueDate value="1995"/>
      </extension>
      <extension url="institution">
        <valueString value="Royal Australian and New Zealand College of Psychiatrists"/>
      </extension>
      <extension url="city">
        <valueString value="Wellington"/>
      </extension>
      <extension url="country">
        <valueString value="NZ"/>
      </extension>
      <extension url="country-name">
        <valueString value="New Zealand"/>
      </extension>
    </extension>
    ...
  </qualification>
  ...

Example instances

Practitioner with educational qualification

Usage info

Usages:

  • Use this Extension: Practitioner resource for use in NZ

Documentation for this format

This structure is derived from Extension

Summary

Complex Extension: Educational qualifications a practitioner has. These may or may not be related to registration information.

  • fullname: string: The full name of the educational qualification
  • shortname: string: A short name of the educational qualification for when space is limited
  • year: date: The year in which the qualification was granted
  • institution: string: The institution that granted the qualification
  • city: string: The city where the institution is located
  • country: string: The country (as iso country code) where the institution is located
  • country-name: string: The country name (as text) where the institution is located
