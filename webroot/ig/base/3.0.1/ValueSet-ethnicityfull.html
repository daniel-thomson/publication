ValueSet: Ethnicity of a person (full)

Official URL: https://nzhts.digital.health.nz/fhir/ValueSet/nz-ethnic-group-code Version: 2.1.0
Active as of 2024-08-14 Computable Name: Ethnicityfull

Ethnicity New Zealand Standard Classification 2005. Ethnicity is the ethnic group or groups that people identify with or feel they belong to.
